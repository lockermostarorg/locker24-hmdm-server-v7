# Bosnian glossary (bs_BA)

Terms used in the Bosnian translation of the Locker24 MDM panel (`core/public/i18n/bs_BA.json` and the `bs_BA.json` of each plugin). Use the same words when adding or changing keys.

## Style

- Bosnian, Latin script, ijekavian: uređaj, obavještenje, vrijeme, prije, primijeni, mjesec, sedmica, historija, tačka.
- Buttons and menu actions are short imperatives: Sačuvaj, Otkaži, Obriši, Uredi, Dodaj.
- Hints, errors and placeholders address the reader with the plural imperative or "da biste": "Unesite naziv", "Kliknite ovdje da biste…". Confirmation questions use the infinitive ("Obrisati grupu '{{groupName}}'?") or "Da li ste sigurni da želite…".
- Kept as-is: MDM, APK, QR, kiosk, Wi-Fi, URL, ID, push, IMEI, SIM, PIN, PUK, Android, Bluetooth, GPS, MQTT, HTTP, TCP, SSID, API, JSON, webhook, launcher, agent.
- Product names are never translated: Locker24, Locker24 MDM, PostBox; Headwind MDM appears only in the About credit line.
- Quotes and punctuation around UI names follow the English source ('Dodaj', «Uređaji», "Zapisnici").
- Display date formats use `dd.MM.yyyy` (and `dd.MM.yy`); time stays `HH:mm`.
- Counts: Bosnian has three plural forms but the panel only has `one`/`other`, so counted phrases use the label form ("konfiguracije: 3", "dodijeljeni tableti: 12", "tableta: 40") instead of a noun after the number.
